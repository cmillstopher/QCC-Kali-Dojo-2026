Enter the Arena

Use these steps to connect your device to the Dojo network and open the station guides. The Arena site is hosted locally, so it may remain available even when the venue’s internet connection is unavailable.

  1. Join the wireless network

    On your laptop or mobile device, connect to Kali Dojo.

  2. Wait for network access

    Leave IP settings on automatic (DHCP). If your device does not connect, ask a volunteer for assistance rather than changing network settings.

  3. Open the Dojo guide

    Scan the QR code on the station signage, or type http://10.50.70.249 into your browser. If a volunteer gave you a printed address, use that.

  4. Choose a station

    Read the station instructions before starting. Follow the scope and safety guidance, and ask a volunteer if a lab target or task is unclear.

Bring Your Own Kali

Option A · Kali virtual machine

Run Kali in your preferred local hypervisor. Keep the VM’s network configuration as instructed by the station volunteer; do not bridge it to networks outside the approved lab.

Option B · Kali on your laptop

A bare-metal Kali installation is fine for attendees who already use one. Save your work and confirm you understand the station’s scope before testing.

Option C · Provided live USB

If you are using a provided live USB, follow the volunteer’s boot instructions. Avoid saving personal credentials or sensitive files on shared or temporary media.

Need a hand? Ask a Red Sands volunteer to help you connect or locate the correct station guide.
Do the activities from inside Kali. Reading on your phone is fine, but the hands-on steps — downloading capture files and running commands like wget, tshark, and nmap — happen in Kali. Once your Kali instance is on the Kali Dojo network, open this guide in Kali’s own browser at http://10.50.70.249 so you can grab files and copy commands right where you’ll run them.

Rules of the Sands

  • Test only systems and targets explicitly provided or authorized for the exercise.
  • Keep scanning and exploitation activity inside the designated lab scope.
  • Do not conduct phishing, social engineering, or testing against venue, attendee, or public networks.
  • Report unexpected behavior to a volunteer and stop if you are unsure whether an action is in scope.
Connectivity tip: If the page will not load, check that you are connected to Kali Dojo and try the URL or QR code shown on the station signage. The local guide does not require internet access.
