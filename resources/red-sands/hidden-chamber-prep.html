The Hidden Chamber · Pre-flight

Prepare for the Lab

A two-minute checklist before you touch a target. The Hidden Chamber is where you apply your Kali skills against deliberately vulnerable, isolated training systems, so a little care up front keeps the lab safe and fair for everyone.

  1. The Chamber
  2. Lab preparation

Before you start

  1. Get your target address and scope from a volunteer. Work only against that address: TARGET=10.50.70.____
  2. Confirm the target VM is up and your Kali is on the lab network, and that you can reach the target and nothing else: ping -c2 "$TARGET"
  3. Take or confirm a clean VM snapshot of your own machine, so you can roll back.
  4. Make a place for evidence: mkdir -p ~/red-sands/evidence, record observation, command, result, and timestamp as you go.
Synthetic data only. Never bridge a vulnerable target to hotel Wi-Fi, the conference network, or the internet, and never store real passwords, tokens, or personal data in your practice files.

Work the cycle

Discover → Validate → Report → Reset

The station page has the full tiered challenges. This is the shape every one of them follows.

1 · Identify

Confirm the target and the scope a volunteer set. Nothing outside it.

2 · Discover

Do service discovery only as directed. Record what you see; don’t guess.

3 · Validate

Confirm a finding using the exercise instructions. No destructive actions, persistence, or credential theft.

4 · Reset

Ask a volunteer to reset the target at the end so the next person starts clean.

The volunteer sets what’s in scope for each exercise. If you’re unsure whether a technique is allowed, stop and ask before running it.
